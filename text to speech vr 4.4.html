How to Use This Tool

  1. 1. Enter API Key

    Click the settings icon (), input key, save & connect. Your key is stored locally.

  2. 2. Select Voice & Configure

    • Use filters or search to find a voice in the left panel.
    • Click a voice to select it (it will appear as a placeholder).
    • Adjust Stability, Similarity, Style, and Speaker Boost.
    • Choose the desired generation Model from the dropdown.
    • Optionally, load or save settings using the Template buttons.

  3. 3. Generate & Manage

    • Enter your text in the main text area.
    • Click 'Generate Audio'.
    • Use the audio player on the right to listen or download.
    • Access, reload, or delete previous generations from the History list.
